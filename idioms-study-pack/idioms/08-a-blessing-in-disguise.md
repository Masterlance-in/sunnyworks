# 8. A blessing in disguise (also: a boon in disguise)

- **English phrase:** A blessing in disguise (also: a boon in disguise)
- **Meaning:** Something that first seems unfortunate but later brings a good result.
- **Hindi gloss:** ऐसा दुख जो बाद में सुखद परिणाम दे.
- **Origin note:** Early printed examples are associated with eighteenth-century religious writing. James Hervey's 1746 hymn is often credited with popularizing the phrase, while some phrase-history accounts point to an earlier 1713 use by Edward Young. The safest summary is that the wording was in print by the eighteenth century; the first-ever spoken use and exact coinage are uncertain.
- **Example sentence:** Missing that train was a blessing in disguise because I met my future business partner on the next one.
- **Memory hook:** Imagine a helpful gift hidden under an unexpected-looking disguise.
